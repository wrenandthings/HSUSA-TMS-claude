# HSUSA TMS guides

Written by Finch on 2026-11-22 at the owner's request ("full and intensive
documentation" and "a walkthrough guide for new users"). They are Claude docs:
the owner can edit, comment, share and export them (Word, PDF, Markdown).

- HSUSA TMS: Getting Started (new users: install, sign in, first tasks, managers' first steps, problems)
  https://claude.ai/artifact/WJq3HFUvWqXUBVqzh7bsiL
- HSUSA TMS: Complete Documentation (roles, qualification rules, every page, quizzes and progress, question banks, settings, data and security, installing and building, technical reference, open items)
  https://claude.ai/artifact/537B7WfNas8RL8L7cPjZW8

The Help page in the app shows copies of both, built in so they work offline:
`src/help/getting-started.md` (everyone) and `src/help/complete-documentation.md`
(Administrators). To update the app after editing a doc: export it from the
doc's Export menu as Markdown and replace the matching file (send it to Finch).

Keep them in step with the app: a change to a rule, page, setting or role
should update the matching section.
